# Support

Vitals is an unofficial fork of Vorssaint, adding Intel Mac support. It is not
affiliated with, endorsed by, or supported by the Vorssaint maintainer.
Please do not report Vitals issues to them — use this repository instead.

Vitals is free and open source, looked after on a best effort basis. There is no support contract and no promised response time, though every issue and question gets read and appreciated.

Before you reach out, the [documentation](#documentation) below answers most of what comes up, and the [troubleshooting guide](docs/TROUBLESHOOTING.md) covers the usual fixes.

## Report a bug

Open the bug report form from the [new issue](https://github.com/rishabhraaj17/vitals/issues/new/choose) page. Please bring your Vitals version from Settings under About, your macOS version, and clear steps to reproduce. The notes on [reporting a useful bug](docs/TROUBLESHOOTING.md#reporting-a-useful-bug) spell out what helps.

## Request a feature

Open the feature request form from the same [new issue](https://github.com/rishabhraaj17/vitals/issues/new/choose) page. Describe the problem you want solved rather than only a specific solution, since that makes the need much clearer. You can also browse and back existing ideas in the [open issues](https://github.com/rishabhraaj17/vitals/issues).

## Report a security issue

Please keep security vulnerabilities out of public issues. The [security policy](SECURITY.md) explains how to share them privately.

## Supporting the project

Vitals is free and it will stay that way. If it is useful to you, a ⭐ on the repository helps other people find it. Contributions of code, translations and bug reports are all welcome, and the [contributing guide](CONTRIBUTING.md) shows where to start.
